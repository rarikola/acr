How picks are scored

  • Every finalist from the daily screen is added on its report date. Entries are never edited or removed, including losers.
  • Entry is the official opening price on the pick date. Reports are published before the open, so this is the first price anyone could trade at.
  • Open picks use the latest quote, delayed about 15 minutes.
  • Each pick closes at the closing price on the first trading day on or after 30 calendar days. Its result is then frozen.
  • vs SPY is the pick's return minus SPY's return over the same window.
  • Returns are for the shares, not for any option trade. Dividends and fees are ignored.

The full pick log is public at picks.json, and every change is in the site's commit history.
